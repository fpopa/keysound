macOS menu bar app

KeySound

Mechanical keyboard sounds for your Mac.

Download .dmg — free, open source

KeySound sits in your menu bar and plays realistic mechanical switch sounds every time you press a key. Key-down and key-up — just like the real thing.

MX

3 Sound Profiles

Cherry MX Brown, Tactile, and Clicky. Each with multiple sample variations.

Vol

Volume Control

Adjust from subtle background texture to full mechanical clatter.

~Hz

Pitch Variation

Randomized pitch per keystroke. No two presses sound identical.

3ms

Low Latency

~3ms audio latency. Sounds arrive with your keystrokes, not after.

↓↑

Down & Up

Separate key-down and key-up sounds for authentic mechanical feel.

☰

Menu Bar

Lives in your menu bar. No dock icon, no windows, no distractions.

Requires macOS 13+. Grants Accessibility permission so KeySound can hear your keystrokes.
